# Reproducibility

## Environment

List operating-system assumptions, compiler or proof-assistant version, dependency lockfiles, and native libraries.

## Formal verification

Provide the clean-checkout commands and identify the exact theorem targets they compile. Record whether proof-hole scans and independent kernels are used.

## Certified computation

Describe the domain, subdivision strategy, rounding mode, certificate format, independent replay, and the exact theorem that consumes the certificate.

## Exploratory computation

List scripts that are informative but not part of the proof. State required packages and random seeds.

## Trust boundary

State what the proof assistant checks internally and what remains trusted externally, including published theorems, compilers, floating-point libraries, interval libraries, and geometric modeling adapters.
